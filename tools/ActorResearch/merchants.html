Merchant identity chooses static stock. Save assignments directly, then rerun the patcher and refresh merchant inventories in game.

Edit stock profiles, rules, and exceptions

SelectionLevel selects existing stock variants using a constant vanilla eligibility benchmark; it is independent of actor PowerTier. MaxEquipmentQuality caps ordinary equipment. GlassPercent is a separate chance per list selection (0–5%); multiple rolls affect the complete shop. Ebony and Daedric are excluded from generated stock. Individual GlassItems offers draw at most one item per refresh; the profile controls their chance. Authored fixed sales remain intact.
