Model and source

Expected melee pressure, in 0.1-second steps. Damage uses loaded game settings and documented fallback coefficients, plus skills, luck, condition, fatigue, armor and an estimated active-block model. Attack cadence, contact rate, power attacks and fatigue costs are explicit assumptions.

Group scenarios focus enemies sequentially; all surviving enemies apply pressure. Other enemies retain their starting fatigue until engaged. Reach and durability are reported, but positioning, equipment wear, staggering, perks, enchantment effects, spells and runtime bonuses are not simulated. Difficulty is represented by each combatant’s outgoing damage multiplier.

Fatigue and power-attack assumptions
Estimated attack cost = base + weapon weight × weight cost; power attacks multiply that cost. Estimated block cost uses the attacking weapon’s weight. Regeneration is set per combatant. These inputs require in-game calibration and currently affect both comparison columns.


Behavioral archetypes
Attack cadence is multiplied by weapon speed. Contact rate is the fraction of attacks that connect, not a native hit-chance roll. Blocking uptime is an assumption about defensive behavior. Native style percentages are decision weights, so they are not automatically converted into these rates. Rate inputs use fractions: 0.6 means 60%.
Native combat-style reference
Loaded effective style records are available for inspection. This milestone does not write native styles. Archer and Mage simulation will arrive with ranged combat and magic.

Weapon class baselines
Absolute Iron-class baselines × material modifiers × class modifiers × individual modifiers. Ordinary enchanted physical stats follow the same model; registered artifacts, scripted and quest equipment retain their exemptions.
Armor slot coverage
Slot rating × material × heavy/light class. Base weight is slot rating × 0.9 for heavy or 0.35 for light; durability is slot rating × 20 or × 10. Shields have an independent slot budget.
Player development estimates
Level 1 uses loaded race, sex, class and passive birthsign bonuses. These controls estimate later development; they do not change in-game skill advancement. Major skills approach mastery at the chosen level; attributes follow the chosen growth assumptions.
Actor tier budgets
Specialty skills reach 100 at tier 5 / level 20. Secondary skills stay lower. Health grows slowly; natural creature attacks grow more sharply. Rare-tier weapon variants use the growth control above and can be acquired from defeated enemies.
Class and creature profiles
Health and attribute multipliers apply to tier budgets. Listed specialties use Specialty; supporting skills use Secondary; other skills use Untrained. SkillMultiplier adjusts training for profiles such as civilians. Trainers receive a floor in their taught skill. Fatigue = Strength + Endurance + Agility + Willpower. Humanoid health multipliers start neutral; there is no forced health/Endurance tradeoff. Large creatures exchange Speed for Health. These class profiles affect NPCs and enemy presets; player presets read the playable class records.
