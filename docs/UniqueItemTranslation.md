# Lore-ranked unique equipment

The configured actor-research OpenAI model (gpt-6-luna) classified 111 registered equipment families / 556 records using verified UESP articles and acquisition context. Research outcomes and consulted sources are saved in UniqueItemResearch.json; full Responses and wiki evidence are cached under artifacts/unique-research. Numerical balance is deterministic in EnhancementBalance, not generated by the LLM.

Physical stats use standard material/class/slot baselines without exponential tier growth. Current catalogue multipliers are 1.0. Tiers govern bounded native enchantment effects; distinct quest states and scripted effects remain intact. The artifact editor exposes these values, source links and comparisons with regular equipment. Seven required quest/signature-power exceptions remain preserved. Coverage is registered official-game equipment, not every possible named mod item.

See [the balance audit](GameplayBalanceAudit.md) for corrected examples, ordinary comparators and verification limits.
